LATEST DELIVERY · 12 NOVEMBER

Sterility by qPCR, and steadier daily work

Live at Optiq

Optiq’s live software version was checked on 13 November 2026; it was installed on 12 November. Everything below is included. The wider requests in the list may still have work to finish.

Results and certificates

  • Record sterility by qPCR. Staff can choose qPCR or USP <71> for each sample in the results table, and the certificate shows the method chosen. Certificates already issued do not change.
  • Fill several samples at once. Dragging a value down the results table saves every sample it covers.
  • Charts follow the latest analysis. The certificate chart follows the newest saved analysis and marks only the assigned peaks. A released certificate’s chart never moves on its own.

Analysis and the lab’s day

  • More on the To analyse list. Each sample shows its status, label claim and recorded identity and purity, with picture icons for identity and purity.
  • Purity follows the analyst. Purity follows the analyst’s newest action, and Undo is available. System or background peaks can be labelled, and the lab can choose to leave them out of automatic integration.
  • See who is on now. Lab administrators have a panel showing who is signed in.
  • Clearer order refusals. When the lab refuses a portal order, the client sees the lab’s reason.

Installed version confirmed through Optiq’s software version record. Release checks used test samples; this page update did not change customer records. Installation does not mean every analytical method or broader request is fully qualified.

LATEST DELIVERY · 7–9 NOVEMBER

Two updates, now installed

Live at Optiq

Optiq’s live software version was checked on 9 November 2026. The latest update includes both weekend release packages. The improvements below are installed; the wider requests in the list may still have additional work to finish.

First update: a smoother path from analysis to certificate

  • Choose what goes on the certificate. Staff can include or leave out instrument charts and mass spectra from the results table.
  • Keep analysis choices together. Improvements cover purity review, splitting peaks, selecting ions and restoring missing chart columns in saved table layouts.
  • Work with mixtures more clearly. The Net Content window supports entering results for each peptide and choosing how identity appears on the certificate.
  • Manage analysis batches more easily. One window brings together adding and removing samples, with searchable sample IDs and selections retained while searching.

Second update: clearer controls and better continuity

  • See, edit and report with clearer controls. Named chart controls separate showing a signal, editing it, using it for purity and including it on the certificate.
  • See where a result came from. A visible note identifies purity entered by hand in the results table, while clearer identity suggestions remain subject to staff review.
  • Keep moving when a background check is slow. The results table and analysis screen no longer wait indefinitely for chart availability checks.
  • Finish the details. Further improvements cover chart labels, sample-list controls, split-peak cleanup and the client-management screen on phones.
