What the labels mean

Completed: our records show the software was released at Optiq or the problem was fixed. Any remaining access, setup, or everyday-use checks are shown under Next step. Partly built: some requested parts are released or built, while others remain unfinished. Built: the feature has been created; release or complete availability still needs confirmation, as shown under Next step. Meeting priority: an unfinished request raised in a recorded meeting or retained meeting notes; these appear first, with Verification Record fonts next. Completed work, deferred requests and exploratory ideas stay separate. Needs checking: a meeting request is recorded, but its current delivery has not been verified. Deferred: agreed later work. Idea: exploratory discussion, with no approved build or delivery promise.
