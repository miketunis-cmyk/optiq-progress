What the labels mean

Planned: recorded, with implementation still ahead. In progress: work under way. Built — release pending: the changes are built, with release at Optiq still to come. Completed: the request is finished or resolved; the expanded details explain how. Verification pending: existing work or reported behavior needs checking before completion or availability can be confirmed.

Needs Optiq input: a lab answer or approval is needed. Partly delivered: some parts are available, with the rest in progress. Needs verification: behavior, readiness or availability still needs checking. Meeting priorities highlights open meeting requests. Deferred requests and ideas are kept for later and are outside the main progress counts.
