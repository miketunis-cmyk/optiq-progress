What the labels mean

Planned: recorded work for future implementation. In progress: work has started and the requested scope remains unfinished. Built — release pending: the capability is built; release, activation or complete availability still needs confirmation. Completed: the recorded scope is closed by specific human acceptance, retained release evidence or an inherited historical claim. Twenty inherited claims still need their underlying receipt linked; these remain closed rather than being reopened. Two additional specific scopes have later closure evidence. Current next steps and details explain the basis; a closed label is not a fresh live test. Status unverified: delivery has not been checked, so no progress stage is guessed.

Waiting and delivery notes are separate from progress: Needs Optiq input identifies a lab decision; Partly delivered means some requested parts are available while others remain unfinished; Needs verification identifies a delivery check. Meeting priorities are recorded unfinished meeting requests. Deferred requests and exploratory ideas are outside the active progress counts.
